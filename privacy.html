Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Buy Sell Trade Sxm is a local marketplace for Saint-Martin and Sint Maarten. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, and account type.
  • Listing details such as title, description, category, price, area, and photos uploaded by the seller.
  • Messages, saved listings, seller ratings, and moderation reports created inside the app.
  • Login information handled by Supabase Auth and, when selected, Google or Apple sign-in.
  • Optional browser notification subscriptions when a signed-in user chooses to receive alerts on a device.

How We Use Information

  • To create accounts and let users publish, manage, save, and share listings.
  • To connect buyers and sellers through marketplace messages.
  • To send optional alerts about marketplace messages and account activity.
  • To moderate unsafe, illegal, fraudulent, or abusive content.
  • To improve the reliability and security of the service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers used to operate the app, such as Supabase for authentication, database, and storage. Public listing information is visible to marketplace visitors.

Advertising and Cookies

This site shows advertising to keep it free to use. We use cookies and similar technologies to run the site, remember your preferences (such as language), and measure and display ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Where required, we ask for your consent before non-essential cookies are set, and we request non-personalized ads until consent is given. You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie choices" link in the site footer.

We may also use Google Analytics to understand aggregate, non-identifying traffic patterns. Analytics is only loaded after consent.

Security

Authentication and user data are handled through Supabase. Access rules are used to limit private data to the correct users and administrators.

Your Choices

You can choose not to create an account. You can turn browser notifications off in the app or in your browser settings. You can also request help with account or listing data by contacting us.
